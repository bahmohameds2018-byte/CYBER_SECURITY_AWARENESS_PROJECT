About SierraShield Cyber Security Hub

SierraShield Cyber Security Awareness Hub was designed specifically to target and empower small businesses in Sierra Leone. Many small businesses lack dedicated IT security departments, making them vulnerable targets for social engineering, malware, and financial scams.

This platform provides hands-on, scenario-based cybersecurity training designed to measure staff awareness, correct common mistakes instantly, and generate official completion certificates.
